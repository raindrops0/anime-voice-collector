# Groups (G)

Start here when an episode has no labels yet. The machine puts clips that sound alike into one group, so you can label many lines at once. Groups shows only the episode picked in the episode menu. Rules shared by every view are in [Labeling basics](labeling.md).

## Labeling a group

1. Tick the lines of one person, or press "Select all". Shift+click ticks every line between the last ticked one and this one.
2. Pick who they are in the bottom bar, or with its keys: a target character (number keys), "Not a target" (Q), or "New target" (N), which makes them a new target character whose name can be filled in later.

While any lines are ticked, these choices label the ticked lines; with none ticked, they label only the line playing.

## Reading a group

- The group's heading names the character the machine guesses most often in it.
- A group may mix several people. Tick and label them in turns.
- A labeled line stays in the group, shows what it was labeled as, and can no longer be ticked.
- Undoing with Z restores the group as it was.

Groups lists only lines still to do: not labeled, not too short, with a voiceprint, not a guess the machine is confident about, and without the "maybe low quality" hint. When most of an episode is labeled, go on in [Scenes](scenes.md) or [Characters](characters.md).
